What is serial section microscopy?

"BrainSaw" is the generic term for our in-house serial section 2-photon systems. Whole brains are embedded in agar, the face of the block is alternately imaged and sliced until the whole sample has been acquired. The sections are generally discarded after the acquisition is complete. The system is not only a labour-saving device, but also produces high-quality 3D data that can easily be registered to a standard atlas. BrainSaw has contributed to over 30 research papers and has been adopted outside of the SWC.

Ecosystem

BakingTray: serial-section acquisition software

BakingTray is modular serial-section acquisition software for MATLAB. It accommodates diverse hardware configurations and acquires images via ScanImage.

Repo

StitchIt: assembling tile scan data to stitched sections

Pre-processes data during acquisition, streams the last stitched section to a web page, initiates stitching automatically when acquisition completes. Includes tools for downsampling and generally batch-processing image stacks. Operations highly parallelised for speed.

Repo

BrainSaw Channel Chooser

This interactive web tool helps BrainSaw users choose the best wavelength and excitation channels for their fluorophores.

Launch

Analysis: BrainGlobe

Our favoured option for analysis of serial section data is the BrainGlobe suite. These Python-based tools are maintained by the SWC neuroinformatics team. BrainGlobe comprises several software packages to access, analyse and visualise anatomical data. The packages are interoperable in order to streamline the process of going from raw data to publication-ready content.
