On this page

    NeuroVision was our first and longest serving BrainSaw system

    Specs

    • Excitation: Mai Tai eHP DS (usable range ~760 to 1040 nm)
    • Laser modulation: Conoptics Pockels cell
    • Detection: Switch to Thorlabs PDA45 SiPMs at Q2 2026. Prior: Hamamatsu R10699 PMTs.
    • Objective: Nikon 16× NA 0.8 (max FOV 1.2 mm on a side)
    • Optical sectioning: PIFOC
    • Scanhead: optically coupled 8 kHz reso/galvo
    • Vibratome: Leica VT1000
