On this page

    Overview

    Our widefield Zeiss Imagers are equipped with Apotomes for optical sectioning. They are ideal for imaging small numbers of slides interactively and have tile scanning ability. Please contact the AMF for an induction if you are unfamiliar with them. See also the manufacturer page.
