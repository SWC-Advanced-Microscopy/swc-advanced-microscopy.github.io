On this page

    Specs

    • Excitation: Mai Tai eHP DS (usable range ~760 to 1040 nm)
    • Laser modulation: Conoptics Pockels cell
    • Detection: Four PMT channels. Hamamatsu R10699 multialkali.
    • Objective: Nikon 16× NA 0.8 water immersion
    • Scanhead: 8 kHz resonant scanner optically coupled to galvo
    • Optical sectioning: PIFOC
    • Vibratome: Leica VT1000

    Filters

    Dichroic pass bands

    • FF635-Di01 — long pass, splits red from far red.
    • DMSP-490R — short pass, splits green from blue.
