On this page

    Specs

    • Excitation: Mai Tai eHP DS (usable range ~760 to 1040 nm) and Coherent Axon-1064.
    • Laser modulation: Conoptics Pockels cell
    • Detection: Four PMT channels. Hamamatsu R10699 multialkali.
    • Objective: Nikon 16× NA 0.8 water immersion
    • Optical sectioning: PIFOC
    • Scanhead: 4 kHz resonant scanner optically coupled to galvo
    • Vibratome: Leica VT1000

    Filters

    Dichroic pass bands

    • Main low pass: FF562-Di03-25x36
    • Long wavelength portion: ThorLabs DMLP638R low pass
    • Short wavelength portion: ThorLabs DMSP490R short pass
