About us

The Advanced Microscopy Facility (AMF) is the imaging core facility of the Sainsbury Wellcome Centre, UCL. Our whole-brain imaging platform focuses on high-throughput, automated microscopy. This is complemented by our histology and spatial transcriptomics services, which use a combination of thin-section slide-scanning and confocal imaging of thick, cleared, tissue. We offer technical support for in vivo functional imaging, as well as designing, building, and maintaining optical systems throughout the Centre. Our tools are developed in the open and used by researchers worldwide.

Location

The Facility is on Level B2 of the SWC, immediately to the left as you come out of the lifts, with a doorbell to the left of the door. You must be laser safety trained to have access.
